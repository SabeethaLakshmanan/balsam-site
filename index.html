One-to-one mentorship

Who we mentor, and how it works for you.

One-to-one means you work directly with a mentor, not in a crowded batch. Every session starts from your goal, looks at your real work, and ends with tasks and deadlines. The next session begins by reviewing what you did.

Students

For school and college students who are unsure about the right course, career or skills to build.

  • Understand your strengths, interests and options
  • Choose a course or career path with reasons
  • Build a study and skill plan, step by step
  • Start small projects that show your ability

You leave with: a shortlist of realistic paths and a 30-60-90 day action plan.

Graduates & job seekers

For fresh graduates and anyone preparing to get a first job or a better one.

  • Resume and profile review with specific fixes
  • Mock interviews with honest feedback
  • Role-wise preparation and application plan
  • Communication and confidence practice

You leave with: a stronger resume, practised answers and a weekly job-search routine.

Working professionals

For people planning a career change, a promotion or a new skill set.

  • Skill gap check against your target role
  • A learning plan that fits your work schedule
  • Guidance on switching fields or growing in your role
  • Feedback on your portfolio and real work

You leave with: a clear target role and a plan to reach it.

Business owners

For startups, small businesses and entrepreneurs who want a clear plan to grow.

  • Business review and gap analysis
  • Marketing, branding and customer guidance
  • AI tools and workflow automation for your business
  • Monthly review and action tracking

You leave with: a growth roadmap and regular accountability.

How a session works

  • Before: you share your goal and any work to review.
  • During: we discuss where you are, what is blocking you and what to do next.
  • After: you get written tasks with deadlines, and we check them at the next session.

FAQ

Good to know.

Straight answers to the questions we hear most.

How do I get started?

Send us a WhatsApp message with your goal or business. We will set up a first call.

What happens on the first call?

We learn about your goal, answer your questions and suggest where to start. There is no pressure to continue.

Do you mentor businesses too?

Yes. Business Growth Mentorship is for startups, small businesses, shops, service providers, institutes and entrepreneurs.

Which business package should I choose?

Growth suits most businesses. Starter is a clear first review and Premium is deeper support. Growth and Premium include AI guidance. We will help you choose.

Do you guarantee a job or result?

No. We promise honest feedback, a clear plan and regular follow-up. Results depend on how the plan is carried out.

Will my information stay private?

Yes. It is used only to guide you and is not shared.
